The business number — two real options

US Mobile · pick one before you start
Correction from earlier: "Add a Line" is NOT a separate account — it's an add-on to your existing personal US Mobile login and billing. If you want the business number fully independent, you need a brand-new account instead.
  1. Quick path — same account: US Mobile app → Add a Line → SIM-only plan. Fast, but the number lives under your personal login and billing.
  2. Full separation — new account: sign up for an entirely new US Mobile account under VTREI's info. Takes longer, but the business number is genuinely independent.

Open the Chase business account

chase.com/business · Business Complete Banking

VTREI qualifies to apply online since it's a single-member-managed LLC — but the document list differs by path. Confirmed directly from Chase's current requirements:

  1. Entity info, either path: Legal entity name: VTREI, LLC EIN: 86-3606031 State: Arizona
  2. Already have: Articles of Organization, EIN confirmation letter, Manager Structure Attachment
  3. Still need to obtain — do this first: Certificate of Good Standing from the Arizona Corporation Commission (required since VTREI is more than a year old)
  4. Worth confirming before you go: whether "AI & I" is filed as a DBA/Trade Name under VTREI — Chase may ask
If applying online: one digital scan of your government ID, taken via your phone camera during the application.

If applying in branch: two forms of ID, at least one primary/government-issued. Bring physical copies of everything above.

Enable Zelle for Business — correctly

Chase Mobile app
  1. In the Chase Mobile app, open the new business account → Zelle settings

  3. This becomes the Zelle tag you give clients for payment
This is the one requirement I confirmed earlier and still holds: Chase requires a different phone number or email for business Zelle than any personal Zelle you already use.
